Scientific Method

1. Observe & Question: Notice a phenomenon.

2. Research: Study existing knowledge.

3. Hypothesis: "If [action] then [result]".

4. Experiment: Test your theory.

5. Conclusion: Your final answer.

Chemistry Properties

Viscosity: Resistance to flow (Thickness).

Malleability: Hammer into sheets (Aluminum foil).

Ductility: Stretch into wire (Copper).

Pure: One type of particle (Distilled Water).

Atoms & Table

Protons (P): Atomic Number (Identity).

Electrons (E): Same as P in neutral atom.

Neutrons (N): Mass Number - Atomic Number.

Groups: 1 (Alkali), 17 (Halogen), 18 (Noble).

Space Systems

Nuclear Fusion: Hydrogen to Helium (Sun's energy).

Terrestrial: Inner, Rocky (M, V, E, M).

Jovian: Outer, Gas Giants (J, S, U, N).

AU: Distance Earth to Sun.

Ecology

Biomagnification: Toxins increase up food chain.

Carrying Capacity: Max population supported.

Photosynthesis: CO2 + Sun -> Food + O2.
