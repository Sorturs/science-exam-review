Full Grade 9 Science Reference

Scientific Method

1. Observe & Question: Notice a phenomenon.

2. Research: Study existing knowledge.

3. Hypothesis: "If [action] then [result]" (theory of what will happen).

4. Experiment: Test your theory.

5. Conclusion: Your final answer.

Chemistry: Properties

Viscosity: Resistance to flow.

Malleability: Ability to be deformed without breaking.

Ductility: Ability to handle tensile force (stretching).

Pure Substance: One element/molecule only.

Homogeneous: Even distribution (Salt Water).

Atomic Structure

Protons: Equals Atomic Number.

Electrons: Equals Atomic Number.

Neutrons: Mass Number - Atomic Number.

Valence: Electrons on the outermost shell.

Metals: Left of the stairs.

Ecology & Symbiosis

Mutualism: Both benefit (+/+).

Commensalism: One benefits, one neutral (+/0).

Parasitism: One benefits, one harmed (+/-).

Biomagnification: Toxin buildup across trophic levels.

Photosynthesis: Sunlight + CO2 -> Oxygen.

Space Unit

Terrestrial Planets: Rocky (Earth, Mars, etc).

Jovian Planets: Gas Giants (Jupiter, Saturn, etc).

Nuclear Fusion: Hydrogen to Helium in the Sun's core.

AU: Distance from Earth to Sun (150m km).

Electricity

Voltage: Energy pressure.

Amps: Energy flow.

Series: Connected end-to-end (one path).

Parallel: Multiple paths for electricity.
